HideLLM

Your assistant, wearing a suit.

You're set up. Open ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, click the HideLLM icon in your toolbar and flip the switch, or press Ctrl+Shift+Z from anywhere.

Privacy

HideLLM makes no network requests of any kind: there is no backend, no analytics and no telemetry, and the source contains no fetch call. Your settings are stored by Chrome and never leave your browser profile. The extension can only run on the assistant sites listed above.

It is a visual disguise. The address bar, your browser history and the network traffic are untouched. It defeats a glance over your shoulder, not an inspection.
